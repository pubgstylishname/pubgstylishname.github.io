Funny PUBG Names That Make Everyone Laugh

Not every PUBG player wants to sound like a fearsome warrior. Some of the most memorable names in the game are the funny ones, the names that make your squad laugh in the lobby and make enemies smile even as they get knocked out. A funny name shows confidence and a sense of humour, and it makes every match more enjoyable. This guide collects the funniest PUBG name ideas, from clever puns to silly self-roasts, so you can pick one that gets a laugh every time.

The secret to a great funny name is that it should be clever, not confusing. The best funny names are short, easy to read, and funny the moment you see them. A name that needs a long explanation is not funny in the kill feed, where players only glance at it for a second. Keep it clean, too. A joke that is rude or offensive can get reported, and nobody wants their account restricted over a name. The funniest names are the ones everyone can enjoy.

  • NoScopeNope — for snipers with a sense of humour
  • SprayAndPray — the classic full-auto approach

  • ReviveMePlease — a cry for help, built into your name
  • LevelOneHelmet — the story of every early game

How to Pick a Funny Name That Lasts

A funny name is only funny if it still makes you smile after a hundred matches. Before you spend a Rename Card, test your joke. Say the name out loud a few times. Show it to a friend and see if they laugh. If the joke needs explaining, it is probably not the one. The best funny names are the ones that work instantly, even for a stranger who sees them once in the kill feed.

Also think about how the name fits your playstyle. A name like "CampingKing" is funniest when you actually love to camp. A name like "PanMan" is perfect if you genuinely enjoy frying-pan fights. When the name matches how you play, the joke lands every single match. And remember the character limit: PUBG Mobile names must be 4 to 14 characters, so long joke names may need trimming. Short and punchy is funnier anyway.

Finally, keep it friendly. The goal is to make the lobby laugh, not to annoy anyone. A clean, clever, funny name is a gift to every match you join. Pick your favourite from this list, or use these ideas to invent your own joke, and enjoy the reactions in your next game.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good funny PUBG name?

Good funny names are short, clean, and instantly understandable, like ChickenDinner, PanMan, LootGoblin, or ProfessionalNoob. The best ones play on something every PUBG player recognises.

Can a funny name get me banned in PUBG?

A clean, friendly funny name is completely fine. Only names that are rude, offensive, or break the game's naming rules can lead to a forced rename or a penalty, so keep your joke friendly.

How do I change my name to a funny one?

You need a Rename Card in PUBG Mobile. Open your profile, tap the edit icon next to your name, type your new funny name, and confirm. The game will use one Rename Card from your inventory.
