PUBG Name Rules and Character Limits Explained

Nothing is more frustrating than designing the perfect stylish name, spending a Rename Card, and then seeing an error message. PUBG has clear rules about what names are allowed, how long they can be, and which characters you can use, and those rules are different on mobile and PC. This guide explains every rule in plain language, so you can design a name that gets accepted the first time and looks great in the game.

Character Limits: Mobile vs PC

The most important rule is the length limit, and it is different on each version of the game:

  • PUBG Mobile: Your name must be between 4 and 14 characters long.
  • PUBG PC (Battlegrounds): Your nickname must be between 3 and 16 characters long.

Every character counts toward the limit, including letters, numbers, spaces where allowed, and symbols. This catches many players out, because a decorated name like "꧁༒☬Viper☬༒꧂" looks short on screen but is actually far longer than 14 characters once you count every symbol. If the game rejects your name for being too long, try a shorter version with fewer decorations, such as "☬Viper☬" or simply "Viper". The clean version usually looks better in the kill feed anyway.

There is also a minimum length. A name shorter than 4 characters on mobile or 3 on PC will be rejected, so single letters and very short tags do not work as player names. Clan tags are separate from player names and have their own shorter limits, which is why most clans use a tag of just two to four letters.

Which Characters Are Allowed?

On PUBG Mobile, you can use English letters, numbers, and a wide range of symbols, including stars, hearts, crowns, skulls, and decorative brackets. However, not every symbol is supported. Very rare characters, some characters from other writing systems, and unusual fonts may not display correctly. When a character is not supported, other players may see a blank box or a question mark instead of the symbol, which makes your name look broken. Stick to common, well-known symbols to be safe.

On PUBG PC, the rules are much stricter. You can use letters and numbers, plus a small set of basic characters such as the underscore. Decorative symbols, fancy fonts, and most special characters are not allowed, and spaces are not allowed either. If you play on both mobile and PC and want the same name on both, design it for the PC rules first, because a name that works on PC will almost always work on mobile too.

One more thing to remember: a name must be unique. If another player already uses the exact name you want, the game will reject it, even if every character is allowed. Adding a number or a small change, like turning "Viper" into "Viper07" or "ViperX", usually solves the problem.

Words and Names That Are Not Allowed

PUBG blocks names that break its community rules. This includes rude or offensive words, hateful language, and names that target other players. It also includes names that try to copy official staff, such as using "Admin", "Moderator", or "PUBG Official" in your name. Names that pretend to be a famous player or streamer are also a bad idea, because they can be reported for impersonation.

Common Reasons Names Get Rejected

If your name change fails, it is almost always one of these reasons:

  • Too long or too short: The name is outside the 4 to 14 character limit on mobile, or 3 to 16 on PC.
  • Already taken: Another player is using the exact same name.
  • Unsupported character: The name contains a symbol or a fancy character the game does not accept.
  • Banned word: The name contains a word the game's filter blocks.
  • No Rename Card: On mobile, you need a Rename Card in your inventory. On PC, you need a Nickname Change coupon.
  • Space in a PC name: PUBG PC does not allow spaces in nicknames, so use a capital letter or an underscore instead.

When a name is rejected, your Rename Card or coupon is not used, so you can try again without losing anything. Change one thing at a time, such as removing a symbol or shortening the name, until the game accepts it. That way you will know exactly what was causing the problem.

Designing a Name That Follows the Rules

Understanding the rules saves you time, Rename Cards, and frustration. Now that you know the limits, you can design your stylish name with confidence and take it into your next match knowing it will work perfectly.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many characters can a PUBG Mobile name have?

Between 4 and 14 characters, including all letters, numbers, and symbols. On PUBG PC, the limit is 3 to 16 characters.

Why was my PUBG name rejected?

The most common reasons are that the name is too long or too short, it is already taken, it contains an unsupported symbol, it includes a banned word, or you do not have a Rename Card in your inventory.

Can I use spaces in my PUBG name?

PUBG Mobile allows spaces in some cases, but PUBG PC does not allow them at all. To be safe on both versions, use a capital letter or an underscore to separate words, like "SilentStorm" or "Silent_Storm".
