PUBG Names in Different Languages

PUBG is played in almost every country on Earth, and that makes names in other languages one of the best ways to stand out. A word that sounds ordinary in your own language can sound powerful, mysterious, or elegant to players from another country — and it is far more likely to be available than a common English name. In this guide, you will find stylish PUBG name ideas in Hindi, Urdu, Arabic, Spanish, Turkish, Korean, Japanese, and more, along with their meanings and tips for using them well.

Why Choose a Name in Another Language?

There are three good reasons. First, availability: English names like "Killer" or "Hunter" are taken thousands of times over, but the same idea in another language is often free. Second, identity: a name in your own language shows pride in where you come from, and your friends will recognise it instantly. Third, style: words from other languages often look and sound striking in the kill feed, especially when combined with a symbol or two. Just make sure you know what the word means before you use it — a word that sounds cool can sometimes mean something silly or rude in its own language.

Stylish Names in Hindi and Urdu

Hindi and Urdu offer many strong words for warriors, kings, and hunters. Here are some popular choices with their meanings.

  • Yodha — warrior
  • Badshah — king
  • Shikari — hunter
  • Sher — lion
  • Raavan — powerful king from legend
  • Jaanbaz — fearless, daring
  • Zalim — fierce, merciless
  • Toofan — storm
  • Aag — fire
  • Khiladi — player

These work beautifully with symbols, for example ༒Badshah༒ or ꧁༺Shikari༻꧂. You can also write them in their native script, such as बादशाह or بادشاہ, but check that the characters display correctly in-game first, since some scripts render differently on different devices.

Stylish Names in Arabic, Turkish, and Spanish

Arabic names often carry a strong, noble feeling. Popular picks include Saif (sword), Asad (lion), Nasr (victory), and Qamar (moon). In Turkish, Aslan means lion, Kartal means eagle, Kaplan means tiger, and Savasci means warrior — all strong choices for a PUBG profile. Spanish offers words like Fuego (fire), Cazador (hunter), Lobo (wolf), Guerrero (warrior), and Relampago (lightning). Each of these is short, easy to pronounce, and carries a clear meaning that fits a battleground.

Stylish Names in Korean and Japanese

Korean and Japanese words are popular with PUBG players because they look clean and modern. From Korean, try Hosu (tiger), Jeonsa (warrior), Bul (fire), Dal (moon), or Geome (sword). From Japanese, try Ryu (dragon), Kaze (wind), Taka (hawk), Senshi (warrior), or Hikari (light). Many players write these in Roman letters, like Ryu or Kaze, so everyone in the lobby can read them. Others use Hangul or Kanji characters, such as 전사 or 龍, for a more stylish look — just test them in-game first to be sure they show up properly on your device.

Tips for Using a Name in Another Language

First, double-check the meaning and spelling with a reliable source before you commit — a small spelling mistake can change the meaning completely. Second, think about readability: if your squad cannot pronounce your name, voice chat gets confusing, so consider a Roman-letter version. Third, mix languages carefully; a word from your language plus an English gaming word, like ShikariX or AslanKing, often works better than a full foreign phrase. Finally, keep the name clean and respectful. Words from another language or culture should be used with respect, never to mock anyone. A name that honours your language and your playstyle is one you will be proud to carry into every match.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use a name in another language in PUBG?

Yes, PUBG supports names in many languages and scripts. Roman-letter versions are the safest choice because every player can read them, while native scripts should be tested in-game first to make sure they display correctly.

What are some good PUBG names in Hindi?

Popular Hindi names include Yodha (warrior), Badshah (king), Shikari (hunter), Toofan (storm), and Aag (fire). They sound strong, are usually available, and look great with symbols.

Will a name in another language be available?

Usually yes. Non-English names are far less common than English ones, so you have a much better chance of getting your first choice without adding numbers or extra characters.
