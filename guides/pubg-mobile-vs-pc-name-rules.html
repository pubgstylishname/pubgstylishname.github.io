PUBG Mobile vs PC Name Rules Compared

PUBG Mobile and PUBG on PC (Battlegrounds) look similar, but their name rules are not the same. Character limits, symbols, rename costs, and clan tags all work differently on each platform. If you play on both, a stylish name that works perfectly on mobile might not fit or display the same way on PC — and a name taken on one platform may be free on the other. This guide compares the name rules side by side so you can pick a name that works everywhere you play.

Character Limits and Length

On PUBG Mobile, player names are limited to 14 characters, which includes letters, numbers, and symbols. That limit shapes everything: a long stylish name with a fancy frame can easily run out of space, so mobile players need short, efficient names. On PUBG PC, the nickname limit is 16 characters, giving you a little more room for symbols and decoration. Two extra characters may not sound like much, but they can be the difference between a name that fits and one that gets cut off. The practical rule is simple: design your name to fit the 14-character mobile limit, and it will fit on PC too. That way, you can use the same stylish identity on both platforms.

Symbols and Stylish Characters

Both versions support many Unicode symbols, but they do not render them identically. PUBG Mobile has wide support for decorative symbols and fancy fonts, and most players see them correctly on modern phones. On PC, support is also broad, but some decorative characters may display differently depending on your system fonts, and very ornate symbols can look unclear on a smaller in-game font size. A symbol that looks great on your phone might look like a blank box on a teammate's PC. The safest approach is to test your name on both platforms before committing. Stick to widely supported symbols like ★, ♛, ༒, ⚡, and the ꧁༺ frame, and avoid the most exotic characters if you play on both.

How to Change Your Name on Each Platform

On PUBG Mobile, you change your name with a Rename Card. You can earn Rename Cards from missions, events, and the Royale Pass, or buy them from the in-game shop with UC. Open your inventory, tap the Rename Card, type or paste your new name, and confirm. On PUBG PC, you change your nickname from your in-game profile or through your platform account settings, and the change is usually tied to a nickname change item or a limited number of free changes. The costs and steps change with updates, so always check the current in-game store or settings before you plan a rename. On both platforms, your new name must be unique — if another player already has it, you will need to change it slightly.

Clan Tags, Titles, and Display Differences

Clan tags are another difference. In PUBG Mobile, clan names and tags appear next to your name in many screens, and clans are a big part of the social game, so players often design their personal name to match their clan's style. On PC, clan and team display works differently and is less central to the everyday lobby experience. PUBG Mobile also shows your name with your frame, title, and avatar in more places, so a stylish name gets more visibility there. On PC, the kill feed and lobby list are the main places your name is seen. If you are building a brand as a player or creator, the mobile version gives your stylish name more chances to shine.

Which Platform Is Better for Stylish Names?

For pure style, PUBG Mobile has a small edge: the community loves stylish names, symbols are widely used, and Rename Cards are easy to understand. For flexibility, PC has a small edge: the 16-character limit gives you more room, and the larger screen makes fancy names easier to read. The best strategy for players on both platforms is to choose one name that fits the mobile limit, uses widely supported symbols, and reads well on any screen. Generate a few options, test them on both platforms if you can, and pick the version that looks clean everywhere. Your name is your identity in both games — make it one you are proud to carry into every match.
