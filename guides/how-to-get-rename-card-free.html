How to Get a Rename Card Free in PUBG Mobile

You have found the perfect stylish name, but there is one problem: you already used your free Rename Card, and a new one costs 180 UC in the shop. Before you spend real money, it is worth knowing that PUBG Mobile gives out Rename Cards for free more often than most players realise. Special events, the Royale Pass, missions, and seasonal celebrations all offer chances to earn one without paying anything. This guide covers every legitimate way to get a Rename Card free, so you can save your UC for something else.

First, a quick reminder about how Rename Cards work. A Rename Card is a one-time-use item that lets you change your in-game name. Every new account gets one free card at the start. After that, each name change needs another card. Cards you earn from events go straight into your inventory, and you can use them whenever you like.

Your First Free Rename Card

If you are a new player, check your inventory first. PUBG Mobile gives every new account one Rename Card for free, usually as part of the early login rewards or the new player missions. Many players use it within their first few days, without realising it was their only free one, and then wonder where it went. If you have not used it yet, you already have what you need. Open your inventory, find the Rename Card under the "Items" or "Treasure" section, and use it to change your name.

Free Rename Cards from Events

Events are the best source of free Rename Cards in PUBG Mobile. The game runs new events almost every week, and several of them include a Rename Card among the rewards. Here is how to make the most of them:

  • Check the Events tab daily: Open the Events section from the lobby every day and look for missions that reward a Rename Card or a Rename Card fragment. Some events give the full card directly, while others give fragments you collect over several days.
  • Complete daily missions: Many events ask you to play a certain number of matches, get kills, or survive for a set time. These are things you would do anyway, so the card is almost free for playing normally.
  • Watch for anniversary and festival events: Big celebrations, such as the game's anniversary, New Year, and major festivals, often have the best rewards, including Rename Cards. These events usually last a few weeks, so there is plenty of time to finish them.
  • Collect event tokens: Some events let you earn tokens by playing matches, which you can then exchange in the event shop for a Rename Card. Always check the exchange shop before an event ends, or your tokens may go to waste.

The key is consistency. A player who checks events every day will earn far more free rewards than someone who only opens the game on weekends. Even five minutes a day is enough to complete most daily event missions.

Royale Pass and Season Rewards

The Royale Pass is another reliable way to earn a Rename Card. Some seasons include a Rename Card in the free track of the pass, which means you can earn it without buying the Elite Pass. Other seasons place it in the Elite track, which requires a purchase, but the free track is always worth checking first. As you play matches and complete weekly missions, your pass rank increases, and you collect the rewards along the way.

Season rewards work in a similar way. At the end of each season, players receive rewards based on their rank and activity, and Rename Cards have appeared in these rewards in past seasons. Even if a Rename Card is not in the current season's list, keep playing and levelling up, because the reward list changes every season and the card comes around regularly.

Plan Before You Rename

Because Rename Cards are not unlimited, it pays to plan before you use one. Pick your new name carefully, test it with a stylish name generator, check that it fits the 14-character limit, and make sure the symbols display correctly. Ask a friend what they think. A name you love today should still feel right in a few months, because earning your next free card might take a while.

Getting a Rename Card free in PUBG Mobile is mostly about patience and playing regularly. Check your events, finish your missions, and keep an eye on the Royale Pass, and you will earn one sooner than you think. Your perfect name is worth the wait.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I really get a Rename Card for free in PUBG Mobile?

Yes. Every new account gets one free Rename Card, and more can be earned from events, the free track of the Royale Pass, and seasonal rewards. No purchase is needed if you are patient and complete the missions.

How much does a Rename Card cost in the shop?

A Rename Card costs 180 UC in the in-game shop. It is a one-time-use item, so each name change needs a new card.

Are websites offering free Rename Cards safe?

No. Websites and apps that promise free Rename Cards or free UC outside the game are scams. Only trust rewards given inside PUBG Mobile itself, and never share your login details with unofficial sites.
