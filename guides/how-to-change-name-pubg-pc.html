How to Change Your Name in PUBG PC

PUBG PC, also known as PUBG: Battlegrounds, is the original battle royale that started it all on desktop. Your nickname follows you into every match, appears on the kill feed, and shows up on the leaderboard after every game. If your current nickname no longer fits your style, or you simply want a fresh start with a cooler identity, you can change it. The process on PC is a little different from PUBG Mobile: instead of a Rename Card, you use a Nickname Change item bought with G-Coin. This guide explains exactly how it works.

Before you start, it is worth knowing that your PUBG PC nickname is tied to your game account, not to Steam itself. Changing your Steam display name will not change your in-game nickname. The only way to change it is through the in-game store, using the official Nickname Change coupon. That also means the name rules are set by the game, so your new name must follow them or the change will be rejected.

Step-by-Step: Changing Your Name on PC

Here is the full process from start to finish:

  1. Launch PUBG: Battlegrounds on your PC and wait for the main lobby to load.
  2. Click on "Store" in the top menu of the lobby.
  3. Go to the "Items" tab and look for the "Nickname Change" coupon.
  4. Buy the coupon with G-Coin. It usually costs around 500 G-Coin, though prices can change with updates.
  5. Open your inventory and find the Nickname Change coupon you just bought.
  6. Click "Use" on the coupon, type your new nickname, and confirm.
  7. Your new name takes effect right away and shows in your next match.

If you already own a Nickname Change coupon, you can skip the shop and go straight to your inventory. The coupon is a one-time-use item, so each name change needs a new one. There is no cooldown on PC name changes beyond the cost of the coupon itself, but buying a new coupon every time can add up, so choose your name wisely.

How to Get G-Coin

G-Coin is PUBG PC's premium currency, and you buy it with real money through Steam or the in-game store. The Nickname Change coupon is only sold for G-Coin, not for BP, the free currency you earn by playing. If you do not want to spend money, keep an eye on special events and the Survivor Pass. Occasionally, events offer a free Nickname Change coupon as a reward, although these are rare. Some players also get one from promotional bundles, so check the store during big updates and seasonal events before you pay full price.

PUBG PC Name Rules to Know

PUBG PC nicknames must be between 3 and 16 characters long. You can use letters and numbers, and a limited set of symbols. Unlike the mobile version, the PC version is stricter about fancy fonts and decorative characters, so most heavily styled names will not work. The game also blocks names that are already taken, names that are offensive, and names that try to copy official staff or famous players. If your name is rejected, try a simpler version or add a number or a short word to make it unique.

Spaces are not allowed in PUBG PC nicknames, which is an important difference from some other games. If you want a two-word name, use a capital letter to separate the words, like "SilentStorm", or an underscore, like "Silent_Storm". This keeps your name readable in the kill feed and on the leaderboard, where there is not much space to show off long names anyway.

Picking a Great PC Nickname

On PC, your nickname appears next to your gameplay in a game where communication and reputation matter. A clean, memorable name helps teammates remember you and makes your highlights look good on streams and clips. Short names with strong words work best: "Viper", "GhostRider", "NightWolf", or "IronFist". Avoid names that are hard to pronounce or spell, because your squad needs to call you out quickly in voice chat during a fight.

Changing your name in PUBG PC is quick once you have the coupon. Buy it from the store, use it from your inventory, and your new identity is live in minutes. Take the time to pick something you will be proud to see on the winner's screen, and enjoy your fresh start on the battlegrounds.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to change my name in PUBG PC?

You need a Nickname Change coupon from the in-game store, which usually costs around 500 G-Coin. Prices can change with updates, so always check the store for the current price before buying.

Can I change my PUBG PC name for free?

There is no permanent free way to change your nickname on PC. Free coupons are occasionally given out during special events or in the Survivor Pass, but they are rare, so most players buy the coupon with G-Coin.

Why was my new nickname rejected?

Common reasons are that the name is already taken, it is shorter than 3 or longer than 16 characters, it contains a space or an unsupported symbol, or it breaks the game's naming rules. Try a simpler variation of the name.
