Best Symbols for PUBG Names

Symbols are what turn a plain PUBG name into a stylish one. The right symbol can make your name look royal, deadly, mysterious, or just plain cool — and the wrong one can make it unreadable. With thousands of Unicode characters available, knowing which symbols work best in PUBG saves you time and Rename Cards. In this guide, you will find the most popular symbol styles, what each one means, and how to combine them with your name for a clean, premium look.

Crown and Royal Symbols

Crown symbols are the most popular choice for PUBG names, and for good reason — they say "king" without using the word. The classic choices are ♛, ♔, ♕, and ༒. Names like ♛KingSlayer♛ or ༒Badshah༒ look powerful in the lobby and the kill feed. Royal symbols work best with names about kings, legends, bosses, and champions. They also pair well with gold and orange themes, which match PUBG's own style. If you want your name to feel like it belongs to a champion, start with a crown.

Star, Fire, and Lightning Symbols

Stars, fire, and lightning symbols add energy to a name. Stars like ★, ☆, ✦, and ✧ give a clean, premium shine — ★GhostRider★ is a classic example. Fire symbols like ♨ or the flame style fit aggressive players, while lightning symbols like ⚡ and ϟ suit fast rushers and high-damage players. These symbols are simple, bold, and display correctly on almost every device, which is why they are among the safest choices. A single star or lightning bolt at each end of your name is often all you need.

Skull, Cross, and Dark Symbols

Dark symbols give a name a dangerous, mysterious edge. Skulls like ☠ and 💀, crosses like ✖ and ✝, and shadow-style marks like 卍-style decorations are popular with players who want an intimidating profile. Examples include ☠DeadHunter☠, ✖SilentKiller✖, and ࿐Ghost࿐. These work especially well for sniper and stealth-style names. One warning: keep dark symbols tasteful and avoid anything that could be read as hateful or offensive. PUBG moderates names, and a symbol choice that crosses the line can get your name changed by force.

Fancy Brackets and Frames

Brackets and frames wrap your name in a stylish border and are the most used decoration in PUBG. The most popular frames are ꧁༺Name༻꧂, 『Name』,, 《Name》, 〖Name〗, and ♛Name♛. The ꧁༺ style is the most famous PUBG frame of all — it looks ornate and instantly recognisable. Bracket frames work with almost any name, from cute to deadly, and they keep the name itself easy to read because the decoration sits at the edges. Try your name in two or three different frames and pick the one that looks best on your screen.

How to Copy and Use Symbols in PUBG

Using symbols is easy. First, generate or copy your styled name from a stylish name generator — just tap to copy the full name, symbols included. On mobile, open PUBG, go to your inventory, use a Rename Card, and paste the name into the field. On PC, paste it into the nickname field in your profile. Before you confirm, check every character carefully: if any symbol shows as a blank box or a question mark, remove it and try a simpler one. PUBG has a character limit for names, so very long decorated names may not fit — if you get an error, shorten the name or drop a symbol. Finally, remember that less is more. One frame or one pair of symbols looks stylish; three different symbol styles in one name looks cluttered.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which symbols work best for PUBG names?

Crowns (♛, ༒), stars (★, ✦), lightning (⚡, ϟ), and frames like ꧁༺Name༻꧂ and 『Name』 are the most popular and reliable. They display correctly on most devices and make your name look stylish without hurting readability.

Can I copy and paste symbols into my PUBG name?

Yes. Copy the styled name from a generator, then paste it into the rename field in PUBG Mobile or the nickname field on PC. Always preview it first so no symbol shows up as a blank box.

Are symbols allowed in PUBG names?

Most Unicode symbols are allowed, but PUBG has a character limit and blocks some characters. Keep your decoration simple and avoid anything offensive so your name is not flagged.
