What to Do If Your PUBG Name Is Taken

You finally found the perfect PUBG name, you type it into the rename screen, and the game says it is already taken. It happens to almost every player, because millions of people share the same good ideas. The good news is that a taken name is not the end of the story — it is the start of a better one. With a few smart tricks, you can keep the name you love, make it even more stylish, and end up with something more unique than the original. Here is exactly what to do.

First, Do Not Panic or Settle

The worst thing you can do is give up and pick a random name you do not like, or add a long string of numbers that looks like a default username. Your PUBG name is your identity in every match, and you will see it hundreds of times. It is worth five extra minutes to get it right. Keep your favourite name as the base and work through the tricks below one by one. In most cases, one small change is all it takes to make a taken name available again — and the changed version often looks better than the original.

Add a Symbol or a Stylish Frame

Symbols change a name just enough to make it unique in the system while keeping it identical to your eyes. If "GhostRider" is taken, try ꧁༺GhostRider༻꧂, ༒GhostRider༒, 『GhostRider』, or ★GhostRider★. A frame around your name is the most popular fix because it looks intentional and premium rather than like a compromise. You can also add a single symbol at the start or end, like ⚡GhostRider or GhostRider♛. Test each version in the rename screen until one is accepted, and check that every symbol displays correctly on your device before you confirm.

Try a Stylish Font

Stylish Unicode fonts are another powerful fix. The same letters written in a fancy font count as a different name in the game. "GhostRider" in a script font becomes 𝓖𝓱𝓸𝓼𝓽𝓡𝓲𝓭𝓮𝓻, and in a bold gothic font it becomes 𝔾𝕙𝕠𝕤𝕥ℝ𝕚𝕕𝕖𝕣. Use a stylish name generator to convert your name into several fonts and try each one. Keep readability in mind: pick a font that still looks clear in the kill feed and that your friends can recognise. A stylish font can turn a frustrating "name taken" message into the coolest-looking name in the lobby.

Change the Spelling or Add a Meaningful Twist

Small spelling changes often unlock a taken name. Try swapping a letter, doubling a letter, or using a synonym: "GhostRider" could become "GhostRyder", "GhostRiderX", "TheGhostRider", or "GhostRiderOP". Meaningful numbers work well too — your lucky number, your birth year, or your squad number, like GhostRider07 or GhostRider99. Avoid random long numbers, which look unfinished. You can also add a word from your language, your favourite weapon, or your clan tag: "Kar98GhostRider" is far more personal and far more likely to be free than the plain version. Combine two tricks at once — a spelling twist plus a symbol — and almost any name becomes available.

When to Pick a Completely New Name

Sometimes the best move is to let the name go. If your favourite name needs so many changes that it no longer looks like itself, it is time for a fresh idea. Go back to what made you like it — was it the word "Ghost", the sniper theme, or the sound of it? Build a new name around that same feeling instead of forcing the old one. A name generator can give you dozens of fresh options based on your favourite word, and one of them will feel right. Remember, the goal is a name you love, not a name you settled for. The right name is out there — and once you claim it, it is yours.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I take a PUBG name that is already used by someone else?

No, PUBG names must be unique, so you cannot claim a name another player already has. You can create a variation with symbols, a stylish font, a spelling twist, or an added word to make it your own.

Do symbols make a taken PUBG name available?

Yes, in most cases adding symbols or a frame changes the name enough for the game to accept it. Try styles like ꧁༺Name༻꧂ or ★Name★ and test each version in the rename screen.

How do I change my name in PUBG Mobile?

Use a Rename Card from your inventory. You can earn Rename Cards from missions and events or buy them with UC in the shop. Type or paste your new name and confirm — the game will tell you right away if it is taken.
