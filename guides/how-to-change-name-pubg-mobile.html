How to Change Your Name in PUBG Mobile

Your in-game name is the first thing other players notice about you in PUBG Mobile. It appears on the kill feed, in the lobby, on the leaderboard, and next to every chicken dinner you win. If you picked a random name when you first started playing, or if your style has simply changed, you can change it. PUBG Mobile lets you rename your character, but you need a special item called a Rename Card to do it. This guide walks you through the whole process step by step, so you can switch to a fresh, stylish name without any confusion.

The first thing to understand is that changing your name is not free every time. When you create your account, PUBG Mobile gives you one free Rename Card, which most players use early on. After that, each name change costs one Rename Card, and you can buy more from the in-game shop with UC. That is why it is worth choosing your new name carefully before you confirm the change.

Step-by-Step: Changing Your Name

Changing your name in PUBG Mobile only takes a couple of minutes once you have a Rename Card in your inventory. Follow these steps exactly:

  1. Open PUBG Mobile and go to the main lobby.
  2. Tap on your avatar or profile icon in the top-left corner to open your profile.
  3. Tap the small pencil or edit icon next to your current name.
  4. Type your new name in the text box that appears.
  5. Tap "OK" or "Confirm" and the game will use one Rename Card from your inventory.
  6. Your new name will appear immediately in the lobby and in your next match.

If the game shows an error, it usually means one of three things: you do not have a Rename Card, the name is already taken, or the name contains characters the game does not allow. PUBG Mobile names must be between 4 and 14 characters long, and some special symbols are not supported. If your stylish name uses fancy fonts or symbols, test it first. Some decorative characters display fine on your own screen but show up as boxes or question marks for other players, which is not the look you want.

Where to Get a Rename Card

If you have already used your free Rename Card, there are a few honest ways to get another one. The most direct way is the in-game shop, where a Rename Card costs 180 UC and can be found under the "Treasure" or "Items" section. Keep an eye on special events too. PUBG Mobile regularly runs missions, anniversary events, and Royale Pass rewards that include Rename Cards. Some events let you earn one for free by completing daily missions or collecting event tokens. The Royale Pass sometimes includes a Rename Card among its rewards as well, so check your pass progress before spending UC.

Another smart tip is to wait for a sale or a bundle. From time to time, the shop offers discounted crates or packs that include a Rename Card along with other items. If you are not in a rush, waiting a week or two can save you UC. And remember: there is no official way to change your name for free once your free card is used, other than earning one through events. Any website or video that promises a free name change outside the game should be ignored.

Choosing a Name You Will Love

Before you spend a Rename Card, pick a name that will still feel right months from now. A good PUBG Mobile name is short, easy to remember, and easy to read in the kill feed. Long names full of symbols can look messy and are hard for friends to search for. Try combining a word you like with a clean symbol or two, such as "ShadowKing", "꧁༒☬Viper☬༒꧂", or "Silent•Storm". Avoid names that are rude or offensive, because they can be reported and may get your account restricted. Keep it fun, keep it clean, and make it yours.

What Happens After You Rename

Changing your name in PUBG Mobile is a simple process once you know where everything is. Grab a Rename Card, pick a stylish name you love, and show it off in your next match. Good luck, and may your new name be the one everyone remembers after the final circle.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change my name in PUBG Mobile for free?

Yes, once. PUBG Mobile gives every new player one free Rename Card. After you use it, each name change needs another Rename Card, which costs 180 UC in the shop or can sometimes be earned free from events and the Royale Pass.

How long can my PUBG Mobile name be?

Your name must be between 4 and 14 characters long. Some special symbols and fancy fonts count differently or may not be accepted, so always test your name before confirming the change.

Why does my name change show an error?

The most common reasons are that you have no Rename Card in your inventory, the name is already taken by another player, or the name contains characters the game does not allow. Try a slightly different name or check your inventory first.
