Frequently Asked Questions

Is this stylish name generator free?

Yes. Every tool on this site is completely free, works in your browser, and needs no sign-up.

Will these stylish names work in PUBG Mobile?

Most of them will. PUBG Mobile accepts many Unicode symbols, but some special characters are blocked. If a name is rejected, try a simpler style from the list.

How do I use a stylish name in PUBG?

Generate a name, tap Copy, then open PUBG, go to your profile, and use a rename card to change your name. Paste the copied name in the name box.

Can I use these names on PUBG PC too?

Yes. PUBG: Battlegrounds on PC also accepts most Unicode characters. The same rules about length and banned words apply.
